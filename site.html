About

Built for Business.
Rooted in Philadelphia.

The Enterprise Lab — powered by Philly Builds AI — is Greater Philadelphia’s dedicated platform for connecting corporate innovation leaders with local enterprise tech companies. We exist because Philly has all the raw ingredients of a world-class B2B tech hub. What’s been missing is an organizing force to bring it to life.

Philadelphia is America’s best microcosm of real business. Diverse industries, top-tier talent, proximity to major markets — and no Silicon Valley echo chamber to distract from solving actual customer problems. This is our edge. Enterprise tech is Philly’s white space to own.

The Enterprise Lab is not an accelerator. We don’t promise deals. We create the conditions for meaningful discovery, learning, and curated connection through roundtables, showcases, and content. It’s all designed to drive innovation and support a thriving enterprise tech ecosystem. Local roots run deeper with a shared commitment to sustainable success.

What We Do

From Discovery
to Problem Solving.

A four-phase program that builds real connections — from understanding what the market needs to putting corporate and builder communities to work together.

01

Phase One

Validate & Connect

We start by listening. One-on-one interviews and surveys with corporate innovation leaders and enterprise builders help us understand priorities, map the local community, and design programming that actually matters.

02

Phase Two

Engage the Community

Roundtables and showcases create the first high-quality touchpoints — bringing corporate leaders and enterprise tech founders into the same room to exchange ideas and build trust, focused on problem solving.

03

Phase Three

Curate Connection & Learning

Programming deepens with fireside chats from Philly’s enterprise tech luminaries, curated corporate-startup matchups, and best-practice roundtables designed around the issues participants actually care about.

04

Phase Four

Build Business Together

Collaborative programming includes hackathons, pilot facilitation, and the Enterprise Tech Showcase — a capstone event that demonstrates Philadelphia’s earned identity as the country’s leading hub for enterprise B2B innovation.

FAQ

Common Questions.

Is the Enterprise Lab an accelerator?

No. The Enterprise Lab is not an accelerator and does not promise deals or funding. We facilitate high-quality discovery and connection between corporate innovation leaders and qualified enterprise tech companies through events and other programming. What happens next is up to the parties.

Who should take the Corporate Innovators Survey?

CIOs, CTOs, CISOs, and operational leaders at Greater Philadelphia companies actively shaping technology and AI strategy. The survey takes about 5 minutes and directly shapes our programming. Take it here.

What is the Enterprise Builders Directory?

A curated, living database of 300+ Greater Philadelphia B2B and enterprise tech companies — organized by solution category, stage, and ideal customer profile. It’s the primary matchmaking reference for corporate buyers exploring local vendors.

What kind of startups do you focus on?

The Enterprise Lab highlights B2B tech in all categories — enterprise apps (yes, including SaaS), cybersecurity solutions, robotics, analytics, and more. If it helps businesses run, it has a home here. We want commercial-ready and revenue-generating startups (there are lots of other communities that can support the “ideation” stage of startups) that businesses can work with as pilots and customers.

How does this relate to PACT, the Chamber, or Ben Franklin?

The Enterprise Lab is complementary to and supported by all of these groups — and more! We want to shine the spotlight on innovative enterprise tech builders and experts for — and with — peer business communities, economic development initiatives, investors and corporate discovery labs.
